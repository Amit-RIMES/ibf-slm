These steps can reduce harm before a hazard strikes — keep them in mind year-round, not only during warnings.
Home and family
  • Store at least three days of safe drinking water (one litre per person per day)
  • Keep a charged phone, a torch, and spare batteries at home
  • Put key documents (ID, insurance, medical records) in a waterproof bag
  • Agree on a family meeting point if you get separated
Know your area
  • Find your nearest evacuation shelter and know two routes to reach it
  • Save local emergency numbers in your phone
  • Learn whether your home is in a flood zone, on a steep slope, or near the coast
Community
  • Check on elderly or unwell neighbours after a hazard warning
  • Share alert information with those who may not have phone access
  • Keep livestock supplies and fodder stocked ahead of forecasted events
